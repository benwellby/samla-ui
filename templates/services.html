Process

How a project runs.

The same five stages apply whether the site is a courtyard or a county park.

  1. 01

    Listen

    Site walks, interviews and workshops with the people who use the place.

  2. 02

    Understand

    Surveys of trees, soils, water, movement and habitats.

  3. 03

    Design

    Options tested with the community, then developed to construction.

  4. 04

    Build and return

    Site supervision, then visits at one, three and five years.
