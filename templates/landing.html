The programme

Three rotations, each with a partner as your mentor.

  1. 01 / Months 1 to 8

    Survey and brief

    Join an engagement team. Walk sites, run workshops and write the brief with residents.

  2. 02 / Months 9 to 16

    Design

    Develop a scheme from concept to technical drawings, with ecologists and engineers alongside.

  3. 03 / Months 17 to 24

    On site

    Follow your project into construction. Stand in the park on the day it opens.

Questions

Before you apply.

Ask us something else
Do I need a landscape degree?

No. We welcome graduates of architecture, urban design, geography, ecology and fine art. Show us how you look at places.

Is the role based in Bristol or Glasgow?

Both. You choose a home studio and spend at least one rotation in the other. We pay travel and accommodation.

What happens after two years?

Every graduate from the last four cohorts has been offered a permanent role. There is no obligation to stay.

What does the application involve?

A short form, a portfolio of up to ten pages and a walk with two of us around a public space of your choice.

Hear when applications open.

One email in March. Nothing else.
